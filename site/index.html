    The problem

    Dengue follows the rain.
    Reports follow the hospitals.

    Rain breeds mosquitoes. People fall sick weeks later. Hospitals report it 4–8 weeks late.

    Weather changes weeks before reports do. EpiLag-PH closes the gap.
    1. Week 0Rain and heat breed mosquitoes.
    2. +1–2 weeksMosquitoes multiply.
    3. +2–4 weeksPeople get sick.
    4. +4–8 weeksHospitals report cases.
    5. ThenHospital reports surge.
    6. ThenCases spread.

    How scoring works

    Each team covers about 33,000 people and prevents a share of cases reported 2–9 weeks later: 10–50%, central 30% (Bowman et al. 2016). Following EpiLag-PH beat reacting to reports in 11 of 17 regions, tied in 1, lost in 5.
