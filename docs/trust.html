Trust and sustainability

Built to keep running. Safely, cheaply, for years.

A forecast is only useful if a health office can still run it next season. This page sets out what EpiLag-PH is worth in numbers, how it reaches more areas and users, how it protects data, who keeps it running, and what it costs people and the planet.

Same budget, more cases prevented.

Where the 25% comes from

Two computer players spend the same 10 response teams (₱500,000 each) over 8 turns in each region. One sends teams where reports already passed the epidemic line; the other follows EpiLag-PH's alerts. Each team prevents 30% of the cases among the residents it reaches over the next 2–9 weeks (Bowman et al. 2016, central estimate). EpiLag-PH did better in 12 regions.

Who gains

Provincial, city and municipal health offices get weeks to clean up and stock test kits and IV fluids. Households get a plain-language outlook in Filipino and English. Hospitals see fewer cases at the peak.

What we do not claim

These are replays and simulations, not a field trial. The effect of a clean-up is a literature range (10–50%), and the costs are example values. The pilot below is how we would measure the real effect.

One weekly file in. Plans out, for every area.

Any area that counts cases

The only required input is weekly case counts per area. The data contract (data/contract/) spells out the columns, and the check rejects a bad file before anything is forecast from it.

Low bandwidth, no install

The app opens in a browser and keeps working offline once loaded, for rural health units with weak signal. Content is in Filipino and English.

Plugs into other systems

Every forecast is published as CSV and JSON with column definitions, and alerts as RSS and calendar feeds, so a DOH dashboard or a newsroom can pull them without us.

Every city and municipality in about a minute.

A full retrain = features, then 3 quantile models with conformal calibration for each of 4 horizons. Real province case histories, copied out for the larger sizes; weather columns of the right shape. Timed on a laptop (Apple silicon, CPU only, no GPU). Reproduce with python scripts/scale_benchmark.py; results in reports/scale_benchmark.csv.

More users cost nothing

Pages and data are static files served from a content delivery network. A thousand health offices or a million residents load the same files; there is no server to overload.

Small downloads

All the forecast data for 104 areas is about 1.6 MB, loaded once and then kept for offline use.

One model, many areas

All areas share one model per horizon, with population as an input, so a new area needs no new model, only its weekly counts.

4 · Data privacy and security

Nothing about any patient. Nothing to steal.

Designed so that the safest data is the only data it needs.

Counts only

EpiLag-PH uses one number per area per week. No names, addresses, ages or case IDs ever enter it, and the data contract check rejects any column it does not know, so a patient line list cannot slip in. That keeps it clear of the personal and sensitive personal information covered by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Small areas, careful handling

The one privacy risk in counts is a very small number in a very small area. Barangay figures are estimates shared out from city totals, and barangay-level weekly data from a partner should stay with the health office, not be published, until it is aggregated.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics. The browser stores only two conveniences on the device (whether the guide was seen and a sort order), and the game's tutorial flags. The only outside request is for the Google Fonts typeface.

A small attack surface

A static site has no database, login or server code to break into, and is served over HTTPS. Dependencies are pinned in lockfiles, and every change runs the tests before it can be merged.

5 · Long-term maintenance

Cheap to run, hard to break quietly.

The danger for any forecast is drift: it keeps publishing after it stops being right. EpiLag-PH checks itself.

A promotion gate

A retrained model goes live only if, on its latest blind test year, it beats "same week last year", stays within 5% of "same as this week", and its range covers 70–90%. Otherwise last week's forecasts stay up and the maintainers are told.

A clear owner and rhythm

A surveillance officer at a city or provincial unit uploads one file a week; a data analyst reviews the gate each season. About 4 staff hours a week in the pilot.

6 · Ethics and equity

A forecast advises. People decide.

Each risk, and what EpiLag-PH does about it.

RiskWhat EpiLag-PH does
False alarms waste money and trustFalse alarm rates are published: 42–63% of watch alerts for regions and Quezon City in 2019–2025, 46% for provinces in 2019. Watch asks for cheap checks (inspect breeding sites, count test kits); only the stricter outbreak warning asks for a task force.
Automated decisionsNothing happens automatically. Every alert comes with its reasons (SHAP), and the local epidemiologist makes the call. EpiLag-PH supports, and does not replace, DOH surveillance and local judgement.
Under-reporting is unevenReported cases undercount infections (about 4.7× in one Cebu City study), and more so where clinics are far. A budget ranked by reported cases could underweight poorer areas, so the plan shows low, central and high estimates and lets the health office replace the costs and effects with its own.
Stigma for flagged placesBarangay rankings are for health offices. The residents' page gives the whole area's outlook and the DOH 4S steps, never a list of "bad" barangays.
Hidden uncertaintyEvery forecast is a range, the weak weather effect is reported openly, and every claim can be checked against the open data.
7 · Environment

Light to run. Lighter on insecticide.

The biggest environmental effect is not the computer. It is where the spraying goes.

Small compute

No GPU and no large AI model. A full retrain is about a minute on a laptop CPU, even at 1,600 areas (see the timings), once a week.

Static hosting

Files served from a cache use far less energy than a server running all day, and the offline app saves repeat downloads.

Less blanket fogging

The budget plan favours clean-ups and container covers (10–50% effect) over fogging (0–10%), and points the work at the areas that need it. Less spraying means less chemical exposure and slower insecticide resistance in mosquitoes.

All of it is open. Check every claim.

The data contract in data/contract/, the checks in scripts/, the weekly job in .github/workflows/, and every result in reports/.
