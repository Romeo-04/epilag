    How to play

    1. Read the mapTall, red blocks have more cases than usual.
    2. Send teamsYou get 10 for the season. Press + on an area.
    3. End the turnTwo weeks pass. A season is 8 turns.
    4. Be earlyA team cuts cases 2 to 9 weeks later. Arrive 4+ weeks before the peak.
    Round 1 · no modelYou see case reports only. The model you pick before starting stays hidden this round: play it on your gut.
    Round 2 · your modelThe same season again, now with your model's 4-week forecasts on every area. Compare the two scores to see what it was worth.

    After each turn, the Turn report tab lists what changed. Drag the map to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom.
