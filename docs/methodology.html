Methodology · as of 18 Oct 2026

How EpiLag-PH works. From weekly data to a plan.

EpiLag-PH forecasts weekly dengue cases 2–8 weeks ahead for 86 provinces, 17 regions and Quezon City, from recent cases and lagged rain, heat and humidity. It flags areas heading for an outbreak and turns each alert into a checklist and a budget plan.

Names to codes

126 location names matched to 86 official PSGC units; typos fixed (NUEVA VISCAYA, SISQUIJOR, BASILIG).

Consistent units

Cities folded into provinces, NCR split into 4 districts, Maguindanao kept whole for 2016–2020.

Bad values removed

Deaths for 2016–2017 excluded: 586 weeks show more deaths than cases.

Missing stays missing

A week with no report is left empty, never set to zero.

2 · Feature engineering

Rain turns into cases 4–8 weeks later.

So each week of rain enters the model separately, as its own lag. That lag is the "Lag" in EpiLag.

Rain, lagged week by week

Rain in each of the last 12 weeks separately; 4-, 8- and 12-week totals; heavy-rain days.

this week12 weeks ago

Recent cases

Each of the last 4 weeks; 4- and 12-week averages; trend; same week last year.

Heat, humidity, season, population

Lagged temperature and humidity; week of the year as a cycle, so late December sits next to early January; log population, so large and small areas share one model.

Targets

Cases 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks ahead, on a log scale so large outbreaks do not dominate training.

3 · Machine learning

A likely range, not a single number.

Gradient-boosted trees predict a low, middle and high case count; a calibration step makes that range honest.

Model

LightGBM quantile regression: one model per horizon (2/4/6/8 weeks), trained on all areas together.

Fast, accurate on tables, explainable

Range

10th, 50th and 90th percentiles of the coming weeks' cases, sorted so low ≤ middle ≤ high.

Officers plan for the high case

Calibration

Conformalized quantile regression (Romano et al. 2019): hold out the last training year, measure misses, widen the range to cover 80%.

Raw ranges are too narrow on a new year

Explanations

SHAP values grouped into recent cases, season, rain by week, humidity and temperature.

Shows why a forecast rose or fell
4 · Evaluation

Tested honestly. No peeking at the future.

5 · From forecast to action

Every alert comes with a plan.

Thresholds per area and week of year, from earlier years only: usual = the average for that week (±2 weeks); epidemic = that average + 2 standard deviations.

Outbreak warningMiddle forecast above the epidemic thresholdConvene the task force; clean-ups in top barangays; prepare hospitals
WatchHigh forecast above the epidemic thresholdInspect breeding sites; check test-kit and IV-fluid stocks
Above usualMiddle forecast above usualRemind households of the DOH 4S
NormalMiddle forecast at or below usualKeep routine surveillance and clean-ups

Action checklist

Week-by-week steps for each level (today, weeks 1–2, weeks 2–4) and what EpiLag-PH contributes to each, downloadable as an action brief.

Budget allocation

A fractional knapsack ranks area-and-action pairs by cases averted per peso. Effects from Bowman et al. (2016): 10–50% for clean-ups with container covers, 0–10% for fogging. Costs are editable.

Quezon City barangays

The city forecast is shared out by each barangay's share of cases in 2022–2025, because barangay data is yearly only.

6 · Why these choices

Simple, explainable, honestly tested.

Past PJDSC champions won with models like XGBoost and K-means used well, not with deep learning.

Explainable over exotic

LightGBM with SHAP can be defended in front of judges and health officers.

Ranges over single numbers

A health office stocks supplies for the high case.

No peeking

Every result comes from years the model never saw, compared with simple rules, with false alarms and the weak weather effect reported.

All of it is open. Check every number.

Code for each stage in src/, every result as CSV in reports/, and the full write-up in the README.
